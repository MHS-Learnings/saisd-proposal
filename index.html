01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

A place to turn
interest into ability.

SAISD is proposed as a collaborative student society at The University of Lahore, bringing together learners who want to understand artificial intelligence and develop useful skills for a changing world.

The society would connect introductory learning with practical exploration through workshops, peer learning, demonstrations, competitions and collaborative projects. It would welcome students from different academic disciplines, recognising that the strongest ideas often emerge when different perspectives meet.

THE CENTRAL IDEAMake technology understandable, practical and accessible to students.
✳Learn together

Encourage peer-to-peer learning and knowledge sharing.

⌁Practice with purpose

Connect concepts with useful, hands-on activities.
